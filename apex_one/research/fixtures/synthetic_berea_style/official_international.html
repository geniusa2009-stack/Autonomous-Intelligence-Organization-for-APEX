International Applicants: Fall 2027 Entry

Key dates

The application deadline for international students applying for Fall 2027 is November 1, 2026. Applications must be submitted by 11:59 pm Eastern Time.

Admission decisions are released by March 15, 2027.

The enrollment deposit is due May 1, 2027.

Cost and the Tuition Promise

Every admitted student receives the Tuition Promise: the college covers 100% of tuition for all four years.

There is no application fee.

International students are expected to contribute an estimated $1,000 per year toward room, board and fees.

A non-refundable enrollment deposit of $50 is required to confirm your place.

Eligibility

Admission is open to international students from all countries.

Applicants must complete secondary school by June 2027.

Because every student receives the Tuition Promise, applicants must demonstrate financial need.

Testing

Example Labor College is test-optional: you are not required to submit the SAT or ACT.

Non-native English speakers must show English proficiency with a minimum TOEFL iBT score of 80, a minimum IELTS score of 6.5, or a minimum Duolingo English Test score of 110.

Application requirements

  • Completed online application form (required).
  • Official secondary school transcripts are required.
  • Two recommendation letters are required: one from a teacher and one from a counselor.
  • One personal essay is required.
  • The International Student Financial Statement is required.

Labor Program

All students work on campus in the Labor Program for 10 hours per week during the academic year.

Visa

Admitted international students study on an F-1 student visa.

The I-20 form is issued after the enrollment deposit and financial documents are received.
